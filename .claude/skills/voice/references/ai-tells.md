# AI tells: patterns to avoid

Used by the drafter and by `voice-engagement`. If a line matches, rewrite it with a concrete, specific statement or cut it.

## Words and phrases
- delve, tapestry, realm, landscape, navigate (non-literal), embark, journey (non-literal), testament to, underscore, pivotal, crucial, robust, seamless, leverage (as a verb), synergy, holistic, multifaceted, elevate, unlock, empower, foster, harness, game-changer, cutting-edge, ever-evolving, in today's fast-paced world
- "It's important to note that", "It's worth mentioning", "In conclusion", "Ultimately", "At the end of the day", "When it comes to"
- "Let's dive in", "buckle up", "here's the thing", "the best part?"

## Structures
- **Not X, but Y** used as a reflex ("It's not just a tool, it's a revolution").
- **Rule of three** everywhere: three adjectives, three bullets, three-beat sentences.
- **Rhetorical question then answer** ("The result? Growth.").
- **Aphorism endings** that sound deep and say nothing ("Love without structure is a liability."). Test: can you say what it means in plain words, and would anyone disagree? If not, cut.
- **Em-dash stacking** and colon reveals in every paragraph.
- **Symmetric paragraphs** of identical length and rhythm.
- **Throat-clearing openers** that restate the question or the context.
- **Summary closers** that repeat the piece.
- **Hedge stacks** ("may potentially help to somewhat improve").
- Bullet lists where a sentence would do.

## Content
- Claims with no source, number or example.
- Generic examples ("a small business owner", "imagine a company") instead of a real one.
- Balanced "on the other hand" padding with no stated position.
- Emojis or bold in formal writing.
