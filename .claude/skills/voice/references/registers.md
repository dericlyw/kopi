# Register profiles

Pick one at the start. Agents receive the profile with every brief.

## Chairman / board brief (formal)
- Reader: chairman or board. Wants the decision, the risk, the ask.
- Shape: decision sought in the first two lines → background (short) → options with cost and risk → recommendation → next steps with owners and dates.
- Tone: formal, respectful, no hype. Numbers over adjectives. No rhetorical questions, no emojis.
- Length: one page where possible.

## Executive memo / email (formal-concise)
- Reader: senior management. Lead with the point and the action needed.
- Short paragraphs, clear subject line, one ask per email.

## WhatsApp / team coordination (concise)
- One message, few lines. Who does what by when. No greeting paragraphs.
- Plain words, lists only when there are 3+ actions.

## Toastmasters speech or club content (warm)
- Reader/listener: club members and guests. Written for the ear.
- Open with a story or a moment, not a definition. One message, repeated at the end in new words.
- Short sentences, pauses, callbacks. Humour welcome. Speaking time drives length (~130 words per minute).

## Article / LinkedIn post (engaging)
- Hook in the first line, a real story, a clear position, one takeaway.
- No hashtag walls, no "Agree?" endings.
